Descriptive only: no rule or measure of the engine changes. "Hidden albuminuria" = adults >= 20 with diagnosed
diabetes or hypertension, not pregnant, ACR >= 30 mg/g and eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021) >= 60 on a single visit.
